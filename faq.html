Minimums & timing

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the line and the product. Partner Factory Line orders are production runs; Handcraft Line orders can be much smaller. We confirm the minimum for your product before you order a sample.

How long does it take?

Samples usually come first, then production. Handwork takes longer than factory work, and jamdani longest of all. Your quote gives a schedule.

Samples

How much is a sample?

It depends on the product. We confirm the sample fee by email before you pay. The fee is paid in advance by card.

Can I combine the two lines in one product?

Yes. For example, kantha patches by artisans on a jacket made in a partner factory. We coordinate both.

Quality

How do you check quality?

The approved sample and your spec sheet are the standard. We check during production and inspect before packing, and send you a photo report.

Will handcraft pieces be identical?

No. Hand stitching and handloom weaving vary slightly. We show you the range at the sample stage and agree what is acceptable.

Shipping & duties

Who ships the goods?

Revival Global Ltd exports from Bangladesh. Samples and smaller orders go by DHL or FedEx; larger orders by sea or air, quoted individually.

Who pays duties and taxes?

They depend on your country and the product, and are normally paid by the importer. DDP, where duties are paid before delivery, can be arranged on request.

Designs

Who owns my design?

You do. We share your files only with the people needed to make your product.

Can we sign an NDA?

Yes. Ask before you send files.
