Steps & payments

How it works.

Five steps from your first message to delivery. You can stop after the sample.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Tell us what you want to make, which line, and roughly how many. We reply with questions, options and the sample fee.

    Free
  2. 02

    Sample

    The sample is made in the line that fits. You pay the sample fee in advance, by card.

    Sample fee, paid in advance
  3. 03

    Quote

    After you approve the sample, you receive a quote with unit prices, quantities, lead time and payment terms.

    Terms set in the quote
  4. 04

    Production

    Production runs with checks during the run and a final inspection against the approved sample.

    As quoted
  5. 05

    Shipping

    Revival Global Ltd exports from Bangladesh. Courier by DHL or FedEx; sea freight for larger orders. DDP on request.

    Shipping as quoted

Who does what

Three parties, one desk.

REVIVAL MADE desk
Run by MEGURIWA in Japan: your inquiries, coordination and the sample payment. You talk to one team, in English.
Revival Global Ltd
The exporter in Bangladesh: production management, quality checks and export documents.
Makers
The partner factory network for the Partner Factory Line, and women artisans for the Handcraft Line.

Payments

Stated before you pay.

Inquiry
Free.
Sample
A sample fee, quoted before you pay and paid in advance by card.
Production
Payment terms are set out in your quote.
Shipping
Quoted with your order. Duties and taxes depend on the destination; DDP is available on request.

Shipping

Exported from Bangladesh.

Exporter
Revival Global Ltd prepares the export documents.
Courier
DHL or FedEx for samples and smaller orders.
Larger orders
Sea or air freight, quoted individually.
Duties
Set by your country and the product. DDP (duties paid) can be arranged on request.
